Pure Efficiency — Pattern Guide

Level 4 · Flags are already placed. Scan, count, and pick the best chord.

Level 4 removes the flagging decision entirely — the board already has every provable mine flagged. Your only job is to look at the revealed numbers, find the ones that are chord-ready, and click the one that opens the most cells. One scan, one click.

Concept Chord-ready — when a number's flag count equals its value

A revealed number is chord-ready when the count of adjacent flags exactly matches the number's value. That means every mine next to it is already flagged — so every other hidden neighbor is safe. Clicking a chord-ready number opens all those safe neighbors at once.

Spot a chord-ready cell

  1. Look at a revealed number — say a 1.
  2. Count its adjacent flags.
  3. If the flag count equals the number's value, the cell is chord-ready.
  4. Any remaining hidden (unflagged) neighbors will open when you click it.
💡 A chord-ready cell with no remaining hidden neighbors does nothing — skip it and look for one that actually has cells to reveal.

Step For each chord-ready cell, count how many hidden cells it would open

Being chord-ready doesn't mean equally useful. Each chord-ready cell has a different number of hidden neighbors — that's your payoff. Count them and compare.

Compare payoffs

  1. For each chord-ready number, count its hidden (unflagged) neighbors.
  2. That count is the chord's payoff — how many cells open with one click.
  3. Pick the chord-ready cell with the highest payoff.
💡 Two chord-ready cells can share hidden neighbors — chording one might make the other's payoff smaller. Always compare before clicking, not after.
