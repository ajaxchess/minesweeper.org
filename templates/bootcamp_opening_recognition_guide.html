Opening Recognition — Pattern Guide

Level 5 · Find the frontier cell whose neighbors are all satisfied, then open it.

Level 5 boards have mines pre-flagged wherever the numbers force it. Your job is to look at the frontier — the ring of hidden cells touching revealed numbers — and find the one cell that is provably safe. A frontier cell is safe when every revealed number next to it is already satisfied (its flag count equals its value). Click that cell to open a new area.

Concept A satisfied number has all its mines flagged

A revealed number is satisfied when the count of adjacent flags exactly equals its value — every mine it can see is already marked. A satisfied number holds no more secrets: every non-flag hidden neighbor it touches must be safe.

Read the frontier

  1. For each frontier cell, look at every revealed number adjacent to it.
  2. Check whether each of those numbers is satisfied (flags = value).
  3. If every adjacent number is satisfied, the cell is provably safe.
  4. If even one adjacent number is not satisfied, the cell carries risk — skip it.
💡 A "0" is always satisfied — it sees no mines and needs no flags. Any frontier cell adjacent only to 0s and satisfied numbers is always safe.

Step Scan every frontier cell — find the one with all satisfied neighbors

The board has many frontier cells. Most touch at least one unsatisfied number, making them risky. Your target is the rare cell whose neighbors are all satisfied — that cell has pressure zero and is 100% safe to click.

Pick the opening

  1. Walk the frontier. For each hidden cell, check its revealed neighbors.
  2. Mark cells where every neighbor is satisfied — those are your safe candidates.
  3. Among safe candidates, pick the one that will open the most new cells.
💡 A safe cell adjacent to a 0 often triggers a cascade — the click opens a whole patch, not just a single cell. Prioritize these for maximum territory.
