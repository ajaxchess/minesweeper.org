Cut Wasted Clicks — Pattern Guide

Level 1 · Two patterns, one question: which move reveals more?

Each drill board has both a chord opportunity and a safe click opportunity somewhere on it. Your job is to find both, count how many cells each would reveal, and pick the bigger one. This is the habit that separates efficient players from cell-by-cell clickers.

Chord Pattern 1 — Chord-ready number

A chord is when you click a revealed number that already has exactly the right number of adjacent flags. Instead of clicking hidden cells one at a time, one chord click opens all remaining hidden neighbors simultaneously.

How to spot it

  1. Find a revealed number — say a 2.
  2. Count its adjacent flags. If the flag count equals the number, it's chord-ready.
  3. Count the remaining hidden (non-flag) neighbors — that's how many cells the chord will reveal.
💡 A number with zero hidden neighbors left after flagging is chord-ready but reveals nothing — skip it. You want the chord-ready number with the most hidden neighbors.

Click Pattern 2 — Provably safe cell

A cell is provably safe when every adjacent revealed number already has all its mines accounted for by flags. You can click it without guessing — the numbers prove it's clear.

How to spot it

  1. Find a hidden cell on the edge of the revealed area (the frontier).
  2. Check each of its revealed number neighbors. Count that number's flags.
  3. If every adjacent number's flag count equals its value, the hidden cell is safe.
  4. Safe cells often open a flood — count the full opening size, not just the one cell.
💡 If any adjacent number still has unflagged mines to account for, the cell is not provably safe — skip it.

Strategy The key skill — compare and pick the bigger move

Both patterns will be on every drill board. The drill asks: which single move reveals the most cells?
