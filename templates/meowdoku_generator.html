How to use the generator
  1. Choose a grid size (4×4 to 10×10). The number of colored regions equals the grid size.
  2. Select a region color from the palette and paint cells by clicking or dragging.
  3. Every cell must be painted, and every region color must be used at least once.
  4. The generator checks in real time whether your regions produce a logically solvable puzzle — one that can be solved by elimination alone, without trial and error.
  5. When valid, click Play to solve it yourself, or Copy Link to share it.
