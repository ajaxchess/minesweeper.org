Strategic No-Flag — Pattern Guide

Level 3 · Read the numbers, find the safe cell — no flags allowed.

Level 3 strips away all flags. The board shows only revealed numbers and a field of hidden cells. Your job: use pure logic to locate a cell that must be safe, and click it. No guessing — every correct answer is provable from the numbers alone.

Concept The ghost mine — a mine you can prove without placing a flag

The core technique is finding a number that is completely trapped — where every one of its hidden neighbors must be a mine. Once you know where the mine is (even without flagging it), you can use that knowledge to free neighboring cells.

How to find a ghost mine

  1. Find a revealed number on the frontier.
  2. Count its hidden (unrevealed) neighbors.
  3. If that count equals the number's value, every hidden neighbor is a mine.
  4. You now have a ghost mine — a proven mine location, no flag needed.
💡 A 1 with exactly one hidden neighbor is the clearest case — that single hidden cell must be the mine.

Step 2 Use the ghost mine to prove a neighbor is safe

Once you know where a ghost mine is, look at every other revealed number adjacent to it. If that number's mine quota is now fully accounted for by ghost mines, all its remaining hidden neighbors are safe to click.

How to use a ghost mine

  1. Find a number adjacent to your ghost mine.
  2. Count how many mines it still needs (value minus any other known mines).
  3. If the ghost mine accounts for all remaining mines, the other hidden neighbors are safe.
  4. Click the safest one — pick the cell that opens the most area.
💡 You never place a flag — you just hold the ghost mine in your head and use it to unlock adjacent numbers.

Strategy Scan the frontier — find the trapped number, unlock the safe cell

The frontier is the edge between revealed and hidden cells — every hidden neighbor of a revealed number. Scan it systematically:

Trap
Find a revealed number where hidden-neighbor count equals its value.
→
Unlock
Use that ghost mine to satisfy adjacent numbers — their other hidden neighbors are safe.
→
Click
Pick the safe cell that opens the most new area when clicked.

Every correct answer in Level 3 follows this chain: trap → ghost mine → safe click. The only variable is how deep the chain goes before you find safety.
