Effective Chording — Pattern Guide

Level 2 · Two steps: prove the mine, then cash in the chord.

Level 1 gave you boards where flags were already placed — you just had to find the best chord or click. Level 2 removes the pre-placed flags. Now you identify and place the flag first, then chord. The catch: you can only flag a cell when you can prove it's a mine from the current numbers.

Step 1 Find a number whose unflagged hidden neighbors are all provable mines

A hidden cell is a provable mine when the numbers force it — no guessing needed. The clearest case: a revealed number whose only remaining hidden neighbor must be the mine.

How to spot it

  1. Find a revealed 1 (or higher number).
  2. Count its unrevealed, unflagged neighbors.
  3. If that count equals the number's value, every hidden neighbor is a mine.
  4. Flag them — you've just earned the right to chord any number touching those cells.
💡 In Level 2, no mines come pre-flagged. You have to earn every flag with a deduction. At most 2 flags are needed per chord opportunity — if you need more, move on.

Step 2 Flag the mine, then chord the best number for maximum reveal

Once you've flagged the proven mine, look at every number adjacent to that flag. Any number whose flag count now equals its value is chord-ready. Click it to reveal all its remaining hidden neighbors at once.

Pick the best chord

  1. After flagging, find all numbers that are now chord-ready.
  2. For each one, count its hidden (non-flag) neighbors — that's the chord's payoff.
  3. Pick the number whose chord reveals the most cells.
💡 Sometimes the same flag enables multiple chords. The drill scores you on the single best click — the chord with the highest individual payoff.

Strategy Scan every number — pick the flag-then-chord with the highest payoff

Multiple numbers on the board may have provable flags. Each flag enables one or more chords. Your one click should be the chord (after flagging) that opens the most cells.

Scan
Find every number with all-provable unflagged hidden neighbors (≤ 2 flags needed).

→
Pick
Click the number with the highest chord reveal count.

This two-step rhythm — prove → flag → chord — is what separates players who waste flags from those who turn every flag into an instant reveal.
